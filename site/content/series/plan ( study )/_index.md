# My Journey into Physical AI & Robotics

## Introduction

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Jeonbuk National University. I am particularly interested in Physical AI and Robotics, a field that connects artificial intelligence with the physical world.

What fascinates me most about Physical AI is that intelligence is not limited to processing information or making predictions. It extends to understanding real-world environments, making decisions, and translating those decisions into physical actions.

I want to understand how intelligent robots perceive their surroundings, learn from data and experience, and interact with the real world autonomously.

## Why Physical AI?

Traditional AI systems primarily process information in digital environments. Physical AI takes this further by connecting perception, reasoning, and action in physical environments.

I find this interaction between intelligence and the physical world especially interesting. Technologies such as computer vision, sensor fusion, robotics, and reinforcement learning provide opportunities to build systems that can respond to complex real-world situations.

My goal is to develop the knowledge and practical skills needed to contribute to this field.

## Learning Roadmap

I plan to approach Physical AI through four learning stages.

### 01. Computer Science & Mathematical Foundations

I will strengthen my programming and mathematical foundations through Python, C/C++, data structures, algorithms, linear algebra, calculus, and probability.

These subjects will provide the foundation for understanding AI models and robotic systems.

### 02. Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision

I plan to study machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision.

I will focus on understanding neural networks, image recognition, object detection, and reinforcement learning while gaining practical experience with PyTorch.

### 03. Robotics & Control Systems

I will explore robotics fundamentals, including kinematics, dynamics, sensors, actuators, and control algorithms.

I also plan to learn ROS 2 and simulation environments to understand how robots perceive and interact with their surroundings.

### 04. Physical AI Integration & Projects

My final goal is to combine the knowledge gained from the previous stages into practical projects.

I want to explore sensor fusion, robot simulation, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning to develop intelligent systems that can perceive, decide, and act autonomously.

## My Current Starting Point

My previous academic projects have helped me develop an interest in algorithmic problem solving and practical implementation.

In particular, I implemented a Nonogram puzzle solver using Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP). Through this project, I worked with Backtracking, Revise, and AC-3 algorithms and analyzed their performance.

Although this project is not directly related to robotics, it provided valuable experience in problem modeling, algorithm implementation, debugging, and performance analysis.

I hope to build upon these experiences as I explore more advanced AI and robotics technologies.

## Long-Term Goal

My long-term goal is to become an engineer who can connect artificial intelligence with the physical world.

I want to develop intelligent robotic systems that can understand their environments, make autonomous decisions, and perform meaningful tasks in real-world situations.

This series will document my learning process, experiments, projects, and progress toward that goal.

**From understanding intelligence to building intelligent machines.**
